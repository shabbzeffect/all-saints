# Data Request — All Saints Mushililie Academy

**To:** The Principal / Head of Institution, All Saints Mushililie Academy (with Board of Management copy)
**From:** SAINTS Platform Team
**Re:** Information required to populate the SAINTS school & parish management platform
**Date:** 12 November 2026

Dear Principal,

To configure the SAINTS database for All Saints Mushililie Academy accurately, we need the following information from the school. We ask that it be provided **in three waves**, as outlined below, so that the core learner register can be verified and validated before historical data is migrated.

All data will be handled under the Kenya Data Protection Act 2019. We request a named data owner on the school's side and written confirmation that the school has the authority and consent to share this information.

---

## Wave 1 — Samples, volumes & governance

1. A sample export (Excel/CSV) of the current learner register and of the current fee balances, so we can validate the data model before full migration.
2. Record volumes: number of learners, guardians, staff, invoices, payments, and library books.
3. Format preferences and the unique identifiers currently in use (admission number, TSC number, national ID, birth certificate / NEMIS number).
4. Written confirmation of authority to share the data, the purpose of processing, and whether the school is registered with the ODPC (if applicable).
5. The school's go-live target term and training availability; who on staff will administer each part of the platform.

## Wave 2 — Master data

**Institution & legal identity**
- Legal name of school and parish, KRA PIN, KNEC / EMIS / NEMIS school codes, Ministry of Education registration number.
- Physical and postal address, phone and WhatsApp numbers, official email, website.
- M-Pesa paybill or Till number, bank account details.
- Contracted bank details for staff payroll, if payroll is in scope.
- Logo / crest artwork and brand guidelines; official letterhead.
- Board of Management and principal contacts; county / sub-county education office contacts.

**Academic structure & calendar**
- Current academic year and term dates (Term 1, 2, 3), mid-terms, holidays, exam weeks, opening/closing dates for the next 2–3 years.
- Grades offered (PP1–PP2, Grade 1–6, Junior School Grade 7–9), number of streams per grade, and class capacities.
- Class teacher and stream assignments; current enrolment per grade and stream.
- School calendar events for the year (open days, sports, chapel, parents' meeting days, exams).

**Learners (per learner)**
- Admission number, full name, date of birth, gender, birth certificate number, NEMIS / EMIS number, nationality, county / sub-county, home address.
- Grade, stream, date of admission, day or boarding status, previous school, sibling links.
- Medical information: allergies, chronic conditions, NHIF / SHA cover, immunization status, emergency medical consent.
- Passport photo; status (active, transferred, withdrawn, graduated).

**Guardians / parents (per learner)**
- Full name, relationship to learner, national ID number, phone(s), alternate phone, email, occupation, employer, physical address.
- Whether the guardian is the custodial guardian or the fee payer (these are often different people).
- Emergency contact and authorized pickup persons.
- Consent preferences: data protection (Kenya DPA 2019), photo / media use, medical treatment, and preferred communication channels (SMS, WhatsApp, email).

**Staff & HR**
- Full staff list: names, TSC numbers, KRA PIN, designation, qualifications, subjects taught, employment type (permanent / contract / part-time / casual), date of joining, current contracts.
- Bank details for payroll; NSSF, SHIF and Affordable Housing Levy numbers; salary grade or scale and allowances.
- Leave balances and leave types used; emergency contacts; passport photos.
- Current payslip template and payroll cycle (monthly is assumed).

**Finance — fee structures**
- Fee structure per grade and term, broken down: tuition, boarding, transport, meals/lunch, development levy, exam fees, caution money, activity/PTA fees — plus any discounts, bursaries, or sibling rates.
- The current term's fee balances per student (arrears as of a cut-off date).
- M-Pesa paybill statement history; receipt books / serial number ranges.
- Chart of accounts and current budget, if finance leadership is available.

**Boarding (if applicable)**
- Dormitory names, capacity, gender, housemaster/mistress, room lists and current occupancy.
- Exeat rules and pass format; boarding fee breakdown; meal plan; nurse/matron contacts.

**Transport (if applicable)**
- Vehicle registration numbers, capacities, insurance and inspection status; drivers' licences.
- Routes, stops, pickup times, transport fees per route; current student-to-route assignments.

**Library (if applicable)**
- Catalogue: accession number, ISBN, title, author, category, shelf, quantity, condition, purchase date.
- Borrowing policy, current borrowers and overdues, fine rates.

**Discipline & co-curricular**
- Discipline code of conduct; incident categories; historical incident records (may be anonymized).
- Clubs and societies list, patrons, membership, sports fixtures.

**Parish (if the portal covers the parish)**
- Parish council, ministries and group lists; member directory; events calendar; giving, pledge and project records.

## Wave 3 — Historical / transactional data

1. Historical attendance records, if any exist (date, learner, status).
2. Historical assessment marks and report card samples (a blank and a filled sample).
3. KPSEA / KJSEA candidate registration numbers for the relevant cohorts.
4. Historical invoices, payments, receipts and arrears (with M-Pesa transaction references).
5. Promotion history (who was promoted when, repeats, etc.).
6. Lesson plans / schemes of work / timetable data (bell times, periods per day, rooms).
7. Existing notice or message templates used with parents.
